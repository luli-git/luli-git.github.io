Lu Li

Email luli1[at]sas.upenn.edu

I am a fifth year PhD student in the Applied Math and Computational Science (AMCS) program at University of Pennsylvania, where I’m fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yong Chen. Previously, I received a B.A. in Mathematics (Honors) and Computer Science from Macalester College, where I was forturnate to work with Prof. Lori Ziegelmeier, Prof. Shilad Sen, and advised by Prof. Andrew Beveridge and Prof. Susan Fox.

My research focus on LLM and reinforcement learning (RL).
